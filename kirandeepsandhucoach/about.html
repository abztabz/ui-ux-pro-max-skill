Two decades of working with leaders across industries and cultures — distilled into one practical system for showing up with clarity.

Hi, I'm Kiran

I help leaders close the gap between what they know and how they're perceived.

I'm an ICF-ACC certified executive coach, a John Maxwell certified coach and trainer, an HRDC-accredited corporate trainer, and a GRI-certified sustainability trainer. I've spoken twice on TEDx stages on leadership and communication, and over the past two decades I've worked with more than 4,000 leaders across industries including IT, logistics, and education.

My own path into this work came from watching capable, experienced people — people who had absolutely earned their seat at the table — hold themselves back. Not from lack of skill, but because their voice wasn't landing the way they intended it to.

That observation, tested and refined across hundreds of coaching engagements and workshops, became the S.P.E.A.K. Influence Framework™: a structured way to align the inner work of leadership (self-awareness, mindset, emotional intelligence) with the outer expression of it (presence, communication, personal brand).

I don't believe in fixing people. I believe in helping the leader who's already there come through clearly — in the room, under pressure, and on the record.
