1:1 Leadership Coaching

For leaders who want to strengthen their influence, presence, and clarity from the inside out.

Book Your Discovery Call

Deep, reflective work — not generic advice

Through personalised, confidential coaching, we apply the S.P.E.A.K. Influence Framework to your real leadership challenges: the meeting where your idea didn't land, the promotion that came with more scrutiny than support, the conversation you've been avoiding.

This is designed to help you lead consciously, not reactively — building self-awareness, presence, and communication skill together, so change shows up in how you actually operate, not just in how you talk about leadership.

Who this is for

  • Mid-level managers and team leads who are technically strong but want greater influence and visibility.
  • Newly promoted leaders adjusting to increased responsibility, expectations, and scrutiny.
  • Women in leadership who are tired of being talked over, overlooked, or not fully heard.
  • Leaders preparing for senior or high-stakes roles who want to communicate with clarity and presence.

How a coaching engagement works

1
Discovery call. A no-pressure conversation to understand your context, goals, and whether we're the right fit.
2
Framework mapping. We identify which of the five S.P.E.A.K. pillars need the most attention for your specific situation.
3
Coaching sessions. Regular 1:1 sessions applying the framework to live challenges — real meetings, real conversations, real presentations.
4
Sustained growth. Tools and habits that keep working after the engagement ends — clarity becomes the default, not the exception.
