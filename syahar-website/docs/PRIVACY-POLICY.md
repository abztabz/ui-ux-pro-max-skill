## What we collect

- **Account data:** name, email, role (family / caregiver), and password (stored only as a secure hash — we never see or store your actual password).
- **Care-recipient (patient) data:** name, age, city, medical conditions, allergies, medications, blood group, doctor, and a local emergency contact. This is **health data / special-category personal data**.
- **Care records:** caregiver visit reports, vitals, chat messages, and care documents.
- **Billing data:** the plan, amounts, and payment records. **Card details are handled entirely by our payment provider (\[Razorpay/Stripe]); we never receive or store them.**
- **Technical data:** basic logs needed to run and secure the service.

## Why we use it, and our lawful basis

- To provide the caregiving service you ask for (performance of a contract).
- To process health data: **your explicit consent**, given at signup. You can withdraw it, though doing so may mean we can no longer provide care.
- To keep the service secure and meet legal obligations.

## Who can see what

Access is enforced technically, not just by policy:

- A **family member** sees only the care file(s) they are linked to.
- A **caregiver** sees only the families assigned to them.
- Our **coordinators** can see operational data to run the service.
- No family can see another family's data.
